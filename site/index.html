About this mapping

The IGLD85 model water surface is transferred to the terrain to create a continuous inundation outline. Blue water has a mapped connection to the lake.

Building stencils are for visual context. They do not change the hydraulic simulation.

Grey marks disconnected / stranded water (estimated): previously inundated areas with no current mapped connection to the lake. It is estimated from terrain reconstruction, not a modelled pond level.

Aerial imagery is 2021 context and does not show the November 2020 flood.

Area statistics refer to the original map area. The added shoreline provides panning context; flood fill is limited to the modeled domain.

The refined map is a visualization of model and terrain data. Review raw output and quality-control maps before drawing conclusions at individual properties.
