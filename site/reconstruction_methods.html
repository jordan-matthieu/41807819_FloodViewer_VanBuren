Map footprint

The initial and reset view is 2,550 m east-west by 2,260 m north-south in EPSG:32617, at 1 m map-raster resolution. Wider context supports panning. A client point defines the display centre only; it does not define an extraction polygon. Flood mapping is restricted to supported model-domain coverage.

Water states and reconstruction

Native connected heads retain their source values. Outside native connected support, an eight-source compact spatial blend and 3 m Gaussian form the water surface; maximum extrapolation is 150 m. Site-specific model-bed datum and wet/dry output settings are applied by the reconstruction pipeline. No temporal water-level average or invented drainage period is applied.

Blue shows positive terrain depth with a current mapped connection to the lake. Grey shows continuously previously wet terrain with positive present support and no current mapped connection. Its footprint cannot expand into previously dry terrain and its diagnostic depth cannot increase while disconnected. Grey is an estimated mapping state, not a mass-conserving or calibrated pond-storage calculation.

Buildings and shoreline

Original footprints are display stencils, not hydraulic barriers. The optional dark red overlay shows current footprint intersection with active or estimated disconnected hydraulic water before stencils and cosmetic shoreline rounding. Raw and Compare use source-matched contacts. Boundary contact is included; this does not establish damage or water inside a structure.

Cosmetic contours use the shared bounded spatial smoothing. Depth bands and gentle local-depth opacity retain the 65% nominal presentation. Source model levels and terrain elevations are unchanged.

Review limits

DEM datum, barriers, culverts, terrain error and model calibration require independent engineering review. Numerical and browser validation is performed separately for this site; validation of the existing reference viewer is not transferred to this new model area.

Matched 0.10 m scenario unavailable: no cross-domain baseline is substituted. Scenario-comparison support is omitted. Other diagnostics use this site's source simulation.
